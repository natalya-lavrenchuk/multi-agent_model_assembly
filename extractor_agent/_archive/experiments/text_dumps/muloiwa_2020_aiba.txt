Aiba-Edward model
The Aiba-Edward model was introduced by Aiba et al., 1968 and
later adopted by Edward in 1970. The model is an unstructured and
inhibitory model extended from the Monod model. The model introduces the inhibitory constant and an exponential ratio of substrate
concentration and inhibitory constant (Sadhukhan et al., 2016). The
inhibitory constant accounts for the presence of toxic substrate concentration in the bioreactor. With the ability to handle toxic substrate,

was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. Three samples were
evaluated and the Aiba-Edwards model produced R² of 96.5%. Contrary
to reported results by Dey and Mukherjee (2010) on phenol degradation, Mohanty et al., (2018) reported a poor ﬁt on phenol degradation.

The Aiba-Edward model performed well as reported in the literature
above. The model handled toxic substrate and non-toxic substrate with
ease. Dey and Mukherjee (2010) and Mohanty et al., (2018) performed

how the data was collected, handled, and analysed. Although both
authors analysed biomass concentration using a spectrophotometer at
