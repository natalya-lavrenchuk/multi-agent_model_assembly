The Luong model was introduced by Luong in 1987. The Luong model is an unstructured model that describes kinetic growth rate based on substrate concentration only. The model is a generalization of the Monod equation. The model includes a new term, (1) − S n m . The new term is used to describe complete growth inhibition above a critical substrate concentration ( Dutta et al., 2015) . The model can predict substrate limitation at low and high substrate concentration accounted for by the parameter, Sm. The model is capable of predicting maximum threshold substrate concentration ( Dey and Mukherjee, 2010) . The Luong model is defined by equation 16.

The Luong model has been used by a number of author as reported below and when utilized, it has performed well. Ibrahim et al., (2016) reported satisfactory fit of the Luong model in caffeine-degrading bacterium. The OD at 600 nm was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. The Luong model produced R² of 93.3%. Hamitouche et al., (2012) reported satisfactory results in biodegradation of phenol in mixed culture. The OD at 600 nm was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. The Luong model produced R² of 91%. Costa et al., (2014) reported satisfactory fit in the degradation of diethyl‑ketone. The OD at 620 nm was used to measure the bacteria

growth rates. The Luong model produced R² of 98%. Ahmad et al., (2015) reported satisfactory fit of the Luong model in caffeine degradation. The cell dry weight method was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. The Luong model produced R² of 98.43%. Carrera et al. (2014) reported satisfactory fit of the Luong model in suspended and an immobilised biomass systems. The cell dry weight method was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. The Luong model fitted suspended biomass system and immobilised biomass system with R² of 80%, and 92.7% respectively. Abubakar et al. (2015) reported satisfactory fit of the Luong model in Tributyltin-resistant Klebsiella sp. Fird 2 in cadmium media. The Luong model produced R² of 98.9%. The Luong model performed well in describing both toxic and non-toxic substrate as reported in the literature above. The additional new term added to the model made it to be more robust.
