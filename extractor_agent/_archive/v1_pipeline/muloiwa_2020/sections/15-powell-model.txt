The Powell model was introduced by Powell in 1967 ( Powell, 1967 ). The model is a modification of the Monod model. The Powell model introduces the parameter (m), which is the maintenance parameter. The Powell model does not consider substrate inhibition, hence it struggles with describing the lag and death phase (Wu et al., 2018) . The Powell model is defined by equation 14.

In this paper, only 2 literatures were found of the Powell model. Costa et al., (2014) reported poor fit of the degradation of diethyl‑ketone by Streptococcus equisimilis. The OD at 620 nm was used to measure the bacteria growth rates. The model produced R² 72%. On the contrary, Mahanta et al., (2014) reported a satisfactory fit of the Powell in E. coli study. R² was reported to be 99.6% for all the models. The Powell model showed its incapacity to describe growth rate in the presence of cell growth inhibition substrate as reported by Costa et al., (2014).
